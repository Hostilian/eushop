### 2. Privacy "Nutrition Label" Data-Use Disclosures
Under App Store rules, you must declare what data is collected. EUshop implements strict data minimization, meaning we collect only what is legally necessary:
*   **Contact Info (Name, Email, Physical Address):** Collected during signup/seller onboarding. Linked to identity. Used for product delivery and legal identity checks (DSA Article 30).
*   **Identifiers (User ID):** Generated to match buyer accounts to their purchases and chat logs.
*   **Financial Info (Payment details, transaction logs):** Stripe tokenization is utilized. Raw cards are never stored. Platform transactions are kept strictly for DAC7 audit logging.
*   **Location Data (Approximate location):** Optional. Used strictly to locate nearby imports. Never tracked in background or shared with third parties.
*   **Camera:** Optional. Used strictly to capture listing photos for merchants.
*   **Data Tracking:** EUshop performs zero tracking across third-party websites or apps.
