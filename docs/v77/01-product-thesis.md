# EUshop v77 Product Thesis

> **"EUshop is the trusted discovery and commerce layer for authentic regional European food. It helps buyers shop across Europe like locals while helping independent European producers and specialist sellers reach customers across the EU."**

## Core Consumer Promise
> **Shop Europe like a local.**

Discover regional foods from independent European sellers, understand exactly where they come from, and buy them through one trusted marketplace.

## Key Product Differentiators
1. **Geographic Discovery**: Explore specialty regional foods by country of origin (Italy, France, Spain, Germany, Greece, Portugal, Poland, Austria, Belgium, Netherlands).
2. **Producer Identity & Authenticity**: Named European traders (DSA Art. 30 compliant) with PDO/PGI protected geographical indication indicators.
3. **Allergen & Dietary Transparency**: Instant visibility of EU Reg. 1169/2011 14 regulated food allergens on every listing.
4. **Cross-Border Accessibility**: Destination-based VAT transparency and cross-border delivery confidence.
