# EU Consumer Protection Directive 2011/83/EU Audit

**Target Components:** `apps/web/pages/checkout.tsx`, `apps/web/pages/cart.tsx`  
**Compliance Standard:** Mandatory Price Transparency & Perishable Goods Rules  

---

## 1. Compliance Controls Checklist

- **Explicit Order Button Wording**: The checkout button MUST display legally compliant text: `"Pay Now — Order with Obligation to Pay"`.
- **Itemized Total Price Disclosure**: Subtotal, VAT amount, and shipping fee MUST be explicitly broken out prior to final payment button click.
- **Withdrawal Right Exception**: Fresh perishable specialty food items (fresh cheese, raw milk products, meat) are explicitly flagged as exempt from 14-day withdrawal rights under Art. 16(d).
