## 1. Initiative Overview

EUshop Version 121 (v121) implements a high-converting, standalone frontend experience demo at `apps/web/public/v121/`.

### Core Features:
1. **Standalone Visual Identity**: Custom palette (ink/cream `#0F172A` / `#F8FAFC` with deep wine `#881337` accent), Fraunces display typography, and zero-dependency HTML/CSS/JS bundle.
2. **Interactive Trust & Compliance Module**: Embedded FIC 1169 allergen matrix, DSA Art. 30 trader identity verification, and real-time VAT calculator.
3. **Cross-Border Specialty Food Catalog**: Curated demonstration of PDO/PGI certified European products across Italy, France, Spain, and Greece.
