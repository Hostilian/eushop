## 2. Geographic Scope

EUshop operates exclusively within the EU Single Market. Shipments to or from
non-EU territories (UK, Switzerland, Norway) are not currently supported due

## 3. Seller Obligations (DSA Art. 30 + DAC7)

### 3.1 KYBC Verification
Before listing any product, commercial sellers must provide:
- (a) Legal name, registered address, phone, and email
- (b) A copy of an ID document or eIDAS-recognised electronic ID
- (c) Payment account details (IBAN/BIC)
- (d) Trade register entry and number (if registered)
- (e) Self-certification that all products comply with applicable EU law

EUshop will make best efforts to verify this information via official databases
(VIES VAT lookup, national trade registers) before activating listings.

> **COMPLIANCE-REVIEW:** "Best efforts" is deliberately undefined in DSA Art. 30.
> Implement the more conservative reading: verify via VIES + trade register
> lookup before setting kycVerified = true.

### 3.2 Suspension
- **DSA:** Listings are suspended swiftly once incomplete or incorrect data is
  identified. No grace period applies.
- **DAC7:** Sellers who fail to provide required tax data receive two reminders
  before suspension (~60-day grace period per DAC7 guidance).

These are two separate suspension clocks. The more lenient DAC7 timeline does
not apply to DSA-covered enforcement.

### 3.3 Food Safety (EU Reg. 1169/2011)
Sellers are solely responsible for:
- Accurate declaration of all 14 EU-regulated allergens as structured fields
- Full ingredient list in descending order by weight
- Net quantity, nutrition declaration, country of origin, FBO name and address
- Storage instructions
- Compliance with all applicable food safety regulations

All mandatory food information must be visible on the listing **before** the
buyer can add to cart (Art. 14 distance-selling requirement).

### 3.4 DAC7 Tax Reporting
Sellers consent to EUshop collecting and reporting their transaction data to
EU tax authorities as required by Council Directive (EU) 2021/514 (DAC7).
Sellers who exceed 30 transactions or €2,000 consideration in a calendar year
are reportable. Annual reports are submitted by January 31.

## 4. Buyer Rights

### 4.1 Right of Withdrawal (EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU)
Buyers have a **14-day right of withdrawal** from distance purchases.

**Exceptions — the withdrawal right does NOT apply to:**
- Perishable goods (e.g. fresh food, dairy, meat) once shipped
- Sealed goods opened after delivery for health protection or hygiene reasons
- Goods made to the buyer's specifications

### 4.2 Pre-Purchase Information
Before completing a purchase, buyers will see:
- Full product information per EU Reg. 1169/2011
- Total price including VAT and shipping
- Seller identity ("Sold by [Seller Name]")
- Estimated delivery time
- Return policy

## 5. Payments

Payment processing is handled by Stripe Connect. By purchasing on EUshop,
you agree to Stripe's terms of service.

**Platform commission:** 15% deducted from seller payouts before disbursement.

**VAT:** Prices are displayed inclusive of VAT. The applicable rate is
determined by the buyer's destination country and the seller's annual
cross-border EU sales volume (OSS threshold: €10,000/year).

> **COMPLIANCE-REVIEW:** Confirm the platform's deemed-supplier status for
> VAT purposes with a tax advisor before launch. In certain scenarios
> (non-EU sellers, low-value imports), the marketplace may be the deemed
> supplier for VAT.

## 6. Platform Liability

EUshop operates under the limited-liability safe harbor for marketplace
operators under DSA Art. 6, provided we:
- Do not have actual knowledge of illegal content
- Act expeditiously to remove illegal content upon notification
- Clearly attribute listings to named third-party traders

EUshop is not liable for product defects, shipping delays, or seller
non-compliance with food safety regulations. Buyers' statutory rights against
sellers are unaffected.

## 7. Illegal Product Notification (DSA Art. 32)

If EUshop becomes aware that a listed product is illegal, we will notify
affected buyers from the preceding six months. Sellers must cooperate with
any product recall or market-surveillance request.
