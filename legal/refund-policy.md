## 1. Your Right of Withdrawal (EU Law)

Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU), you have the right to
withdraw from a distance purchase within **14 days** without giving a reason.

The 14-day period starts from the day you (or a third party you nominate)
receive the goods.

To exercise this right, notify the seller via the EUshop messaging system or
email legal@eushop.com before the 14-day period expires.

---

## 2. Exceptions — When the Withdrawal Right Does NOT Apply

The 14-day withdrawal right **does not apply** to:

- **Perishable goods** (fresh food, dairy, meat, fish, baked goods) once shipped
- **Sealed goods** opened after delivery where opening makes them unsuitable
  for return for health protection or hygiene reasons (e.g. opened food packaging)
- **Goods made to your specifications** (custom orders)
- **Goods that have been inseparably mixed** with other items after delivery

> **COMPLIANCE-REVIEW:** The seller must clearly state on the listing which
> exception applies. EUshop must display this before "Add to cart" is
> actionable (Art. 14 distance-selling requirement).

---

## 3. Defective or Incorrect Goods

If goods are defective, damaged in transit, or not as described, you have
additional rights under EU consumer law regardless of the withdrawal right:

- **Repair or replacement** at no cost to you
- **Price reduction** if repair/replacement is not possible
- **Full refund** if the defect is significant

Report defects within **48 hours of delivery** via the EUshop platform.
Photograph the damage before opening or discarding packaging.

---

## 4. How to Return

1. Contact the seller via EUshop messaging within the withdrawal period
2. The seller will provide return instructions within 3 business days
3. Return the goods in their original condition and packaging
4. You are responsible for return shipping costs unless the goods are
   defective or not as described

---

## 5. Refund Timeline

Once the seller confirms receipt of the returned goods:
- Refunds are processed within **5 business days**
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- Stripe processing time: 5–10 business days depending on your bank

---

## 6. Disputes

If a seller does not respond or refuses a valid return request, contact
EUshop support at support@eushop.com. We will mediate the dispute.
