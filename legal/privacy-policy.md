## 4. Your Rights (GDPR Arts. 15–22)

- **Access (Art. 15):** Request a copy of your data.
- **Rectification (Art. 16):** Correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure (Art. 17):** Request deletion. We anonymise personal identifiers
  while retaining anonymised order records for tax obligations (up to 10 years).
- **Portability (Art. 20):** Download a machine-readable JSON export from your
  account dashboard or by emailing privacy@eushop.com.
- **Object (Art. 21):** Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Restrict (Art. 18):** Request restriction of processing.
- **Withdraw consent:** At any time for consent-based processing.

To exercise any right: privacy@eushop.com — we respond within 30 days.

You may also lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.
