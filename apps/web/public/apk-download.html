Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't EUshop on the Play Store?
We're currently in a beta phase and distributing the app directly to gather feedback. Once we reach a stable release, we plan to publish on the Play Store.
Is it safe to install APKs from EUshop?
Yes. This APK is signed with our official certificate and hosted on our secure servers. We recommend only downloading APKs from trusted sources like our official website.
How do I update the app?
We'll notify you via email and in-app messages when a new version is available. Simply download the new APK and install it — your data will be preserved.
Can I use my EUshop account on both the app and website?
Yes! Your account, orders, and messages are synced across all platforms. Log in with the same credentials on any device.
