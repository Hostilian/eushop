# EU Wine Digital E-Labeling Rules

## Requirements
1. All wines produced after 8 Dec 2023 must disclose ingredients and nutrition data (off-label via QR e-label permitted).
2. Energy value (E) in kJ and kcal must appear on physical label.
3. E-labels must NOT contain user tracking, marketing, or sales links.
