# EU Data Act (EU 2023/2854) Rules

## Requirements
1. Users and business sellers have the right to access and share data generated by their use of the platform.
2. Data must be made available in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
3. Unfair contractual terms regarding data access are non-binding.
