# EU Toy Safety Rules

## Requirements
1. Mandatory CE marking on all toys intended for children under 14.
2. Specific warnings for toys not intended for under 3s ("Warning. Not suitable for children under 36 months. Small parts.").
3. Manufacturer contact address on toy or packaging.
