# European Accessibility Act (EAA) Rules

## Requirements
1. E-commerce platforms must be accessible to persons with disabilities (EN 301 549).
2. Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, and Robust (POUR) principles.
3. Mandatory accessibility statement published and linked in footer.
