# EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Rules

## Requirements
1. Products with digital elements must provide an SBOM (CycloneDX / SPDX).
2. Mandatory 24-hour reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities to ENISA.
3. Guaranteed security updates for the expected product lifecycle (min 5 years).
