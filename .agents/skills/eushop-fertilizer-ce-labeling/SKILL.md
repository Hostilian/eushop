# EU Fertilising Products Rules

## Requirements
1. Clear declaration of Product Function Category (PFC 1-7).
2. Compliance with maximum contaminant limits (e.g. Cadmium < 60 mg/kg P2O5).
3. CE mark affixed to EU-certified fertilising products.
