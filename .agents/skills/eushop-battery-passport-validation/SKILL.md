# EU Battery Regulation Rules

## Requirements
1. Digital Battery Passport required for industrial batteries > 2kWh and EV batteries.
2. Mandatory carbon footprint performance class labeling.
3. Minimum recycled content documentation (Cobalt, Lead, Lithium, Nickel).
