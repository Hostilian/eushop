# EU Green Claims Directive Rules

## Requirements
1. Total ban on generic environmental claims ("eco", "green", "climate positive") without recognized certification.
2. Ban on claims based on GHG emissions offsetting ("carbon neutral product") without lifecycle proof.
3. Pre-verification of environmental claims by accredited independent verifier.
