# EU Packaging & Packaging Waste (PPWR) Rules

## Requirements
1. Packaging must meet Design for Recycling (DfR) criteria.
2. Mandatory display of packaging material components and sorting instructions.
3. Producer EPR registration for primary and secondary shipping packaging.
