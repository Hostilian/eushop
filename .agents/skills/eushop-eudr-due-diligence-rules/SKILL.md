# EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) Rules

## Requirements
1. Geolocation coordinates (latitude/longitude) of all plots of land where relevant commodities were produced.
2. Due Diligence Statement (DDS) submitted via EU Information System before placing on market.
3. Proof that commodities are deforestation-free (produced on land not subject to deforestation after 31 December 2020).
