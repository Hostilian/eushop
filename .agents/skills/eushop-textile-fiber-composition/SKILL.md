# EU Textile Fiber Labeling Rules

## Requirements
1. Full fiber composition breakdown totaling 100%.
2. Only official fiber names allowed in EU 1007/2011 Annex I.
3. Clear indication if item contains non-textile parts of animal origin (e.g. leather patches).
