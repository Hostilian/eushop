# EU CSDDD Supply Chain Rules

## Requirements
1. Integrated due diligence policy across upstream and downstream supply chains.
2. Mandatory grievance mechanism for affected stakeholders.
3. Adoption and implementation of a climate change mitigation plan aligned with the Paris Agreement.
