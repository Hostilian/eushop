# EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR) Rules

## Requirements
1. Mandatory Unique Device Identifier (UDI-DI) displayed on product detail page.
2. EUDAMED registration of manufacturer and authorized representative.
3. EU Declaration of Conformity and Notified Body approval for non-Class I devices.
