# EU Conflict Minerals (3TG) Rules

## Requirements
1. Importers of 3TG metals above annual volume thresholds must perform supply chain due diligence.
2. Verification that smelters/refiners are listed on RMI conformant lists.
3. Third-party independent audit requirements for high-risk origins.
