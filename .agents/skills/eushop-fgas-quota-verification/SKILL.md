# EU F-Gas Regulation Rules

## Requirements
1. Equipment containing F-gasses must be labeled with chemical name, GWP, and CO2 equivalent weight.
2. Quota verification for HFC importers/sellers.
3. Pre-charged equipment requires seller declaration of certified technician installation.
