# EU Omnibus Price Reduction Rules

## Requirements
1. Any price discount announcement MUST display the prior lowest price applied during a period of at least 30 days.
2. Progressive price reductions (e.g. 10% off then 20% off) must reference the original pre-discount price.
3. Fake "was" prices are strictly prohibited and subject to consumer protection fines.
