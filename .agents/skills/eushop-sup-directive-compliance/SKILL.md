# EU Single-Use Plastics (SUPD) Rules

## Requirements
1. Total ban on 10 specific single-use plastic items (e.g. plastic cutlery, plates, straws, oxo-degradable plastics).
2. Mandatory "Plastics in product" turtle logo on cups, wet wipes, and sanitary items.
3. Tethered caps required on plastic beverage bottles up to 3 liters.
