## Battery Regulation Passport Agent

Enforce EU Battery Regulation (EU 2023/1542).

### Key Responsibilities
- Digital Battery Passport QR code validation for EV and industrial batteries
- Minimum recycled cobalt/lithium/nickel percentage check
- Battery carbon footprint declaration verification
