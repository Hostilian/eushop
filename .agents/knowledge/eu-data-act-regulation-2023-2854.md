# EU Data Act Regulation (EU 2023/2854) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 establishes harmonised rules on fair access to and use of data (Data Act).

## Core Directives
- **Data Access Rights**: Users can obtain data without undue delay, free of charge, and where applicable, continuously and in real-time.
- **Switching Data Processing Services**: Removal of obstacles to effective switching between data processing providers.
