# EU AI Act (EU 2024/1689) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 lays down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (Artificial Intelligence Act).

## Key Transparency Obligations (Art. 50)
- Providers must ensure AI outputs are marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated.
- Users of an AI system that generates or manipulates text/images ("deepfakes") must disclose that the content has been artificially generated or manipulated.
