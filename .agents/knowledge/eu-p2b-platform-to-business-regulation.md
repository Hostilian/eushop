# EU Platform-to-Business (P2B) Regulation

## Overview
EU Regulation 2019/1150 (P2B) — Promoting Fairness and Transparency for Business Users of Online Intermediation Services.

## Key Obligations for EUshop
1. **Terms & Conditions**: Must be written in plain language, durable medium, advance notice of changes (15 days min)
2. **Ranking transparency**: Publish ranking parameter disclosure — must explain what affects product ranking
3. **Differentiated treatment**: If EUshop offers its own products, must disclose any self-preferencing
4. **Access restriction**: If restricting/suspending seller account, must give 30-day notice (unless serious violation)
5. **Complaint handling**: Free internal complaint handling system required
6. **Data access**: Sellers must have access to data about their own sales and buyers who purchased their products
7. **Parity clauses**: Any MFN/parity clauses must be justified publicly

## Ranking Disclosure Required Text
```
EUshop ranks products based on: (1) buyer review score (40%), (2) seller fulfilment rate (30%), (3) product listing completeness (20%), (4) relevance to search query (10%). Sponsored products appear separately and are clearly marked.
```

## 30-Day Notice Template (Account Restriction)
Required fields: Reason, Evidence, Appeal process, Contact for dispute, Appeal deadline.
