# EU Wine Labeling Regulation (EU 2021/2117) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2021/2117 amended the Common Agricultural Policy (CAP) rules making it mandatory to communicate the list of ingredients and the nutrition declaration for wine products.

## Key Rules
- **Physical Label**: Energy value (e.g. `100 ml: E = 310 kJ / 75 kcal`) and allergens.
- **E-Label (QR Code)**: Complete ingredient list and full nutrition table provided digitally without user tracking.
