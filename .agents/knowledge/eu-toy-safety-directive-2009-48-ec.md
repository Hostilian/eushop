# EU Toy Safety Directive (2009/48/EC) Reference

## Overview
Directive 2009/48/EC lays down safety requirements for toys placed on the EU market, ensuring high protection for children against chemical, physical, mechanical, electrical, and flammability risks.

## Labeling Obligations
- CE mark minimum 5mm high.
- Warning text prefixed with "Warning" or "Warnings".
- Precautionary statements easily legible.
