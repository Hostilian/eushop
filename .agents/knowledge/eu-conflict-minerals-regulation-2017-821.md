# EU Conflict Minerals Regulation (EU 2017/821) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2017/821 lays down supply chain due diligence obligations for Union importers of tin, tantalum and tungsten, their ores, and gold originating from conflict-affected and high-risk areas.

## Core Rule
Union importers must abide by the 5-step framework of the OECD Due Diligence Guidance.
