# EU Customs De Minimis and TARIC Rules Reference

## Overview
Under EU VAT and Customs rules, import VAT is charged on all commercial goods entering the EU, regardless of their value.

## De Minimis Limits
- **Customs Duty**: Exemption applies for consignments up to €150.
- **Import VAT**: No exemption (0 EUR threshold since July 1, 2021).
