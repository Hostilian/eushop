# EU Whistleblower Directive (EU 2019/1937) Reference

## Overview
Directive (EU) 2019/1937 on the protection of persons who report breaches of Union law provides protection for whistleblowers in both public and private sectors.

## Key Principles
- **Confidentiality**: Identity of the reporting person cannot be disclosed without explicit consent.
- **Protection against retaliation**: Absolute prohibition on suspension, demotion, discrimination, or legal retaliation.
