# EU Cosmetics Regulation (EC 1223/2009) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 is the main regulatory framework for finished cosmetic products when placed on the EU market.

## Key Rules
- **Responsible Person**: Must be designated within the EU.
- **Safety Assessment**: Cosmetic Product Safety Report (CPSP) required prior to placing on market.
- **CPNP Notification**: Centralised electronic notification before market launch.
