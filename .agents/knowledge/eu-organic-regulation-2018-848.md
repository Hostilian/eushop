# EU Organic Regulation (EU 2018/848) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2018/848 sets rules on organic production and labeling of organic products.

## Mandatory Labeling Details
- Control body code format: `[ISO country code]-[BIO/ÖKO/EKO]-[3-digit code]`
- Place of farming: EU Agriculture, Non-EU Agriculture, or EU/Non-EU Agriculture.
