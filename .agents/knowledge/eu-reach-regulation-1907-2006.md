# EU REACH Regulation (EC 1907/2006) Reference

## Overview
REACH is the European Community Regulation on chemicals and their safe use (Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemical substances).

## Obligations for Marketplaces
- Art. 33: Duty to communicate information on substances in articles (SVHC > 0.1% w/w).
- Annex XVII: Restrictions on manufacture, placing on the market and use of certain dangerous substances.
