# EU Single-Use Plastics Directive (EU 2019/904) Reference

## Overview
Directive (EU) 2019/904 aims to prevent and reduce the impact of certain plastic products on the environment, particularly the aquatic environment.

## Key Measures
- **Market Restrictions (Bans)**: Applies to cotton bud sticks, cutlery, plates, straws, stirrers, balloon sticks, expanded polystyrene food/beverage containers.
- **Product Design Requirements**: Caps and lids to remain attached to beverage containers.
- **Mandatory Marking**: Visible, legible and indelible marking on specified packaging.
