# EU Textile Labeling Regulation (EU 1007/2011) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011 governs textile fiber names and related labeling and marking of the fiber composition of textile products.

## Mandatory Information
- Fiber composition Breakdown (e.g. 80% Cotton, 20% Polyester).
- Indication of non-textile parts of animal origin.
- Multilingual labeling in destination market languages.
