# EU Craft & Industrial GI Regulation (EU 2023/2411) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2023/2411 creates a unified EU-wide framework for protecting geographical indications for craft and industrial products (such as ceramics, glassware, clothing, jewelry, cutlery).

## Protection Scope
- Direct or indirect commercial use of a registered name for comparable products.
- Any misuse, imitation or evocation (e.g. "Solingen style").
