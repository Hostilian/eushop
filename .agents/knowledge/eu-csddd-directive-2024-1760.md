# EU CSDDD Directive (EU 2024/1760) Reference

## Overview
Directive (EU) 2024/1760 on Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence sets rules on obligations for large companies regarding actual and potential adverse impacts on human rights and the environment.

## Key Duties
- Prevent, mitigate, and bring to an end adverse human rights and environmental impacts.
- Establish and maintain a complaints procedure.
