# EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR 2017/745) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2017/745 establishes a robust, transparent, predictable and sustainable regulatory framework for medical devices.

## Key Elements
- **EUDAMED Database**: Central database for medical device registrations, UDIs, certificates, and clinical investigations.
- **UDI Assignment**: Unique Device Identifier required on device packaging and product pages.
