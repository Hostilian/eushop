# EU WEEE Directive (2012/19/EU) Reference

## Overview
Directive 2012/19/EU aims to prevent or reduce the negative impacts of the generation and management of waste from electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE).

## Key Principles
- **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)**: Financial and operational responsibility for end-of-life management.
- **Crossed-out Wheelie Bin**: Mandatory symbol on all EEE placed on the EU market.
