# EU Fertilising Products Regulation (EU 2019/1009) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2019/1009 lays down rules on the making available on the market of EU fertilising products.

## Key Rules
- **Product Function Categories (PFC)**: Organic fertilizers, organo-mineral, inorganic, liming materials, soil improvers, growing media, plant biostimulants.
- **Cadmium Limits**: Strict limits to prevent soil accumulation.
