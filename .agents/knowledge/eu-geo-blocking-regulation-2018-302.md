# EU Geo-Blocking Regulation (EU 2018/302) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2018/302 addresses unjustified geo-blocking and other forms of discrimination based on customers' nationality, place of residence or place of establishment within the internal market.

## Prohibited Practices
- Blocking access to websites based on customer's EU location.
- Automatic redirection to another version without explicit consent.
- Applying different payment conditions based on location of payment account.
