# EU Ecodesign Framework Regulation (ESPR) Reference

## Overview
The Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (EU 2024/1781) establishes a framework for setting ecodesign requirements on specific product groups to improve their circularity, energy performance, and environmental sustainability.

## Key Elements
- **Digital Product Passport (DPP)**: Unique QR code linking to material breakdown and repair guides.
- **Banned Destruction of Unsold Goods**: Mandatory reporting and restriction on destroying unsold textiles/footwear.
