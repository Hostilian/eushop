# EU Battery Regulation (EU 2023/1542) Reference

## Overview
Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 regulates the entire life cycle of batteries placed on the EU market, from design to end-of-life recycling.

## Key Rules
- **Battery Passport**: Accessible via QR code, containing performance metrics, composition, and recycled content.
- **Supply Chain Due Diligence**: Mandatory due diligence policies for raw material sourcing.
