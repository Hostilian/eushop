# EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) Reference

## Overview
The Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (EU 2024/3065) aims to reduce packaging waste, increase reuse and refill options, and make all packaging recyclable by 2030.

## Core Directives
- **Recyclability**: All packaging placed on the EU market must be recyclable (Grade A, B, or C).
- **Recycled Content**: Minimum post-consumer recycled plastic targets for plastic packaging.
- **Excessive Packaging**: Restrictions on empty space ratio in transport packaging (max 40%).
