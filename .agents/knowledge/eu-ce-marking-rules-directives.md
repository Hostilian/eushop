# EU CE Marking Rules & Directives Reference

## Overview
The CE mark is mandatory for products sold within the EEA that fall under specific EU harmonisation legislation (e.g. Low Voltage Directive, Toy Safety Directive, EMC Directive).

## Manufacturer Requirements
- Conduct conformity assessment.
- Create technical file.
- Draft and sign EU Declaration of Conformity (DoC).
- Affix CE mark visibly and legibly.
