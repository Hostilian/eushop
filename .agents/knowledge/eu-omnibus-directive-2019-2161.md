# EU Omnibus Directive (EU 2019/2161) Art. 6a Reference

## Overview
Directive (EU) 2019/2161 introduced Article 6a into Directive 98/6/EC regarding price reduction announcements.

## Core Rule
Any announcement of a price reduction must indicate the lowest price applied by the trader during a period of time not shorter than 30 days prior to the application of the price reduction.
